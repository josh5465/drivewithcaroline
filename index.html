About me

Lessons tailored to you. I customise my teaching to fit your individual needs and guide you to success.

25years teaching in primary schools

Hi, I'm Caroline. I'm a newly qualified driving instructor, and before that I spent twenty-five years working as a primary school teacher. My years in the classroom have helped me fine tune my ability to remain calm under pressure, be patient and understand that everyone learns differently.

The goal is to get you to become a safe independent driver. What I can offer you is a calm and supportive environment in which to achieve this. We will start at a point which is appropriate to you and you alone.

How lessons work

Red, amber, green.

Everyone starts from a different point. Some have never sat in the driving seat whilst others have had lessons before. Some people are confident and excited and others are nervous. We start where you actually are.

We start where you are

The first lesson is about getting comfortable - and figuring out where you are. If you're nervous, that's ok. We can start on quiet roads with minimal traffic, or if you are ready we will move onto more challenging routes. We move at a pace which is appropriate for you. Equally, if you have had lessons before we assess where you are at and start from there.

We build it up, one thing at a time

Junctions, roundabouts, dual carriageways and country roads are all part of driving, but it's the skills of planning, anticipation and awareness of other road users which will help you navigate these safely. You get to decide at the start of each lesson what we're covering, and we move on when you're ready - not when a syllabus says so.

Test ready, and ready for after

Once you've gained the necessary skills and experience the focus shifts towards you driving independently and putting everything you've learnt into practice, making those critical decisions by yourself. By now you will have gained the skills necessary to pass your driving test but, more importantly, become a safe driver for life.

Common questions

Before you ring

I've had lessons before and it didn't go well.

Very common, and usually it's a mismatch rather than a lack of ability. Tell me what happened and what you found hard, and we'll do it differently. Starting again with someone new isn't going backwards.

How many lessons will I need?

Honestly, it depends - the DVSA's own rough guide is around 45 hours with an instructor plus private practice, but people vary enormously in both directions. I'd rather give you a real answer after a few lessons than a made-up one now.

Can I use my own car, or practise with a parent?

Private practice alongside lessons helps a lot, as long as your supervising driver is over 21 and has held a full licence for three years, and the car is properly insured for you. I'll tell you what we've covered so you can practise the right things rather than reinforcing the wrong ones.

What do I need before my first lesson?

A valid provisional licence and glasses or lenses if you wear them for distance. That's it - bring those and we can get going.

I'm an older learner and I feel daft about it. Is that a problem?

Not remotely, and you're in better company than you think. Plenty of people learn in their thirties, forties, fifties and beyond - often because life finally made it necessary. Nobody's judging the timing.
